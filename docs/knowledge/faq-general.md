# General FAQ (DRAFT — replace with FaezSports wording)

What should my child bring? Indoor court shoes, a water bottle, and a reversible or light/dark shirt for scrimmages. Basketballs are provided.

Can parents watch? Yes. There is bleacher seating in the gym. Please stay off the court during sessions.

What happens if a session is cancelled? Weather or facility cancellations are announced in the program WhatsApp group at least 2 hours before start. Cancelled sessions are made up at the end of the program where possible.

How do I pay? Registration is completed and paid online at faezsports.com. We accept credit card through the registration page. E-transfer is available on request; reply here to arrange it.

Is there a sibling discount? Yes, 10% off the second child in the same season. Reply here and staff will apply it.

Is there a waitlist? When a program is full the registration page shows a waitlist button. We contact families in order if a spot opens.
