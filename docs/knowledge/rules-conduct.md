# Player and Parent Code of Conduct (DRAFT — replace with FaezSports wording)

Players arrive 10 minutes before start, dressed and ready. Respect coaches, teammates, opponents and officials at all times. No jewellery on the court. Repeated disruptive behaviour may result in removal from the program without refund.
